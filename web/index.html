Data & API. All of this data is free to download as JSON, CSV or SQLite, with docs and the code that builds it, at github.com/gBlaku/wow-forever-kb.
Where this comes from. Item data is read from the WoW Forever game client's own data tables (build …). Stats and weapon damage are calculated with Blizzard's item-budget formulas and match Wowhead on the items checked. DPS marked with ~ is approximate. Crafting recipes also come from the client data.
Quests and vendors come from the Questie addon's WoW Forever database. Creature and chest loot, with drop chances, comes from the vmangos Classic world database. Both are Classic content, so quests and drops added or changed in WoW Forever aren't listed yet. Use the Wowhead link on each item to check.
Classic data. Blizzard hides many Forever items until a player finds them in-game. Where the item existed in Classic, its name, level and stats are filled in from Classic and marked "Classic data". Damage and armor use the Forever formulas. About 7,500 new Forever items have no data anywhere yet.
Level filter. Each item is matched to the level it is really for: its required level, or for items with no requirement, the level of the quest that rewards it or the creature that drops it (otherwise item level − 5). With your level set, items for more than 2 levels above you are left out unless you tick "Show items meant for higher levels". Weapon and armor rules follow Classic: Hunters and Shamans wear mail from 40, Warriors and Paladins wear plate from 40.
